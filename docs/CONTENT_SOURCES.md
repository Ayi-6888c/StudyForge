Retain source URLs, check dates, reference years and gaps. A historical match is not current admission permission. Ordinary candidates, special schemes, retired service members and exam exemptions may have different rules.

## Expansion requirements

1. Confirm exam type, year, candidate category and registration/assessment/admission organization from provincial policy.
2. Confirm school enrollment, receiving-major scope and extra conditions from the current charter/table.
3. Extract subjects, marks, duration, textbooks and format from the syllabus. Leave unknowns empty; do not invent chapter weights.
4. Build an independent learning adapter and obtain teaching review of questions, answers and marking.
5. Run positive/negative matching, plan regression and annual-change previews; publish after human review.

## News discovery is not a policy database

Two dedicated columns cover Hubei education policy and Hubei Normal University notices. The other 47 schools initially track existing source-page changes; dedicated news columns remain incomplete. This does not scan the entire internet or every archived page.

An initial fetch establishes a baseline, not a reform notification. URL dates are not automatically publication dates. Multiple-year headlines remain review candidates. A changed page does not establish a changed rule or a student's eligibility.
